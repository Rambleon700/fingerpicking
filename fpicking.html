Welcome & Philosophy

Fingerpicking (fingerstyle) is the art of plucking individual strings with the fingers of the right hand instead of a pick. It allows independent bass lines, melody, and harmony simultaneously.

This suite takes you from absolute beginner to advanced independence with visual fretboards, interactive tools, and classic songs.

Core Principle: Always practice slower than you think you need. Speed is a by-product of accuracy and relaxation.

1. Posture, Hand Position & Nail Care

Sitting Position

  • Sit upright on a firm chair.
  • Classical position: left foot elevated, guitar on left thigh, neck angled upward.
  • Folk position: guitar on right thigh is fine for steel-string.

Right-Hand Position

  • Wrist slightly arched.
  • Fingers approach strings nearly perpendicular.
  • Thumb (p) stays in front of the fingers.

7. 12-Week Progress Roadmap

  1. Weeks 1–2: Posture + basic p-i / p-i-m. Use Interactive Fretboard presets.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Full Travis on C-G-Am-F.
  3. Weeks 5–6: First complete song (Dust in the Wind style).
  4. Weeks 7–8: Blackbird – melody + bass independence.
  5. Weeks 9–10: Longer arpeggios + harmonics.
  6. Weeks 11–12: Intermediate song + banjo rolls / light percussion.
